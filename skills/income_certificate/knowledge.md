# Income Certificate — sample guidance (illustrative, not official)

> This knowledge pack is sample content written for the SAGE Seva demo. Real
> requirements differ by state and are set by the issuing authority (usually
> the Tahsildar / Revenue department). Replace this file with the official
> checklist of your state before real use.

## What it is
An income certificate states the annual income of a person or family. It is
commonly needed for scholarships, fee reimbursement, reservation in
education, loans at concessional rates, and welfare scheme applications.

## Documents usually required
- Proof of identity: a government photo identity card.
- Proof of address: ration card, voter ID, or utility bill.
- Proof of income: salary slip, employer certificate, land/pattadar records
  for farmers, or a signed self-declaration of income where no formal proof
  exists.
- A recent passport-size photograph.
- Application form with the applicant's details filled exactly as in the
  identity document.

## Common reasons applications are sent back
- The applicant's name is spelled differently in the form and in the
  attached documents (for example initials vs full surname, or a different
  English spelling of the same name).
- Date of birth differs between documents.
- Income stated in the form does not match the income proof.
- Missing proof of address or missing income proof.

## Tips for operators
- Type the name exactly as in the identity document that is attached.
- If documents disagree, get the person to confirm which spelling is
  correct, and note the variant spellings in the remarks.
- Read the filled form back to the applicant in their own language before
  submitting.

## Processing
- Typical processing time is about 7 to 15 working days, varying by state.
- The certificate is typically valid for one financial year; check locally.
